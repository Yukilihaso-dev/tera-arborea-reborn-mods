# Tera Arborea Reborn Mods

Tera Toolbox mods for Tera (Arborea Reborn).

## arborea-visual-cleanup

- Removes hand glow
- Removes blue nostrum flashes
- Removes flying dash blur
- Removes the screen vignette
- Removes camera shake
- Applies your saved video settings at character select

## arborea-auto-potions

- Automatically uses HP and MP potions from your inventory
- Separate HP and MP thresholds, with combat-only option
- Primary potion plus up to seven ordered backups per resource
- Supports combined HP+MP potions
- Pauses while loading, dead, mounted or trading
- Respects shared potion cooldowns
- Local settings window
